# User-Defined Design & Development Rules (Permanent Storage)

## 1. CRITICAL: No Subtitle Badges / Eyebrow Pills Ever
- **NEVER** add Subtitle Badges, Eyebrow Pills, or pulsing dot pill tags (e.g. `[● Verified Courts...]`, `[● Smart Booking...]`, etc.) above or below section titles, cards, or hero headlines on ANY page or website.
- Go straight to the main headline (`<h1>` / `<h2>`) and content without floating pills or badges above the titles.

## 2. Signature Button Component
- Always prioritize and use the user's signature `<Button>` component (`cyber-btn` / `cyber-btn-secondary` with polygon clip-path cuts and glowing borders).
